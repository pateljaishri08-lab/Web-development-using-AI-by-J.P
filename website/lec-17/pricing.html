Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our pricing and services

❓ Can I customize a pricing plan?

Absolutely! All three plans can be customized based on your specific requirements. Contact our sales team to discuss modifications to any plan, additional features, or hybrid solutions that better fit your project scope and budget.

❓ What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), bank transfers, and flexible payment arrangements. For Enterprise plans, we can discuss custom payment schedules and terms.

❓ Is there a deposit or down payment required?

Yes, we require a 50% deposit to start the project, with the remaining balance due upon completion. For longer projects, we can arrange milestone-based payments for easier cash flow management.

❓ What happens after my support period ends?

After the included support period, you can extend support at discounted rates (typically $500-$1000/month depending on plan). We're here to help your application grow with your business long-term.

❓ Do you offer refunds if I'm not satisfied?

We're confident in our work and strive for 100% client satisfaction. If there are issues with the delivered project not meeting agreed specifications, we'll work to fix them at no additional cost. We don't offer refunds, but we ensure unlimited revisions until you're happy.

❓ How long does each plan typically take?

Startup plans: 2-3 weeks, Professional plans: 4-6 weeks, Enterprise plans: custom timeline (typically 8-12 weeks+). Timelines depend on project complexity, revisions, and your availability for feedback.

❓ Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes! If you start with a Startup plan and need more features, you can upgrade to Professional or Enterprise. The upgrade cost will be adjusted based on the work already completed.

❓ What's included in the post-project support?

Support includes bug fixes, minor updates, security patches, and technical assistance. Major new features or significant modifications may require additional fees. We'll provide detailed support scope during project kickoff.

❓ Do you work with agencies and resellers?

Yes! We offer special pricing and white-label solutions for agencies, development firms, and resellers. Contact our sales team to discuss partnership opportunities and volume discounts.

❓ What about maintenance and hosting costs?

Project costs are separate from hosting and maintenance. We can help set up hosting (typically $50-$500/month depending on needs) or work with your existing hosting provider. Maintenance plans start at $500/month for ongoing support.
